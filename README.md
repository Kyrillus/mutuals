# Mutuals

the personal people crm for the agentic era

## Features

* Sync all contacts from linkedin / whatsapp / email / instagram
* Graph visualisation based on background / industry -> bigger node size for better connections, smaller size for weaker
* Semantic Search of network
* Synergy Reminders -> "you just met X and he needs Y - Z in your network has Y. You could introduce both"
* Property fill-out by voice 

## Properties 
* Name
* Job (Founder, Investor, Operator, Student, Community builder, etc.)
* City
* Company 
* Age (birthday reminder?)
* Area of interest
* 
* Additional information 


## Diffirentiator of other tools
* It should be proactive and give you reminders and help you stay in touch with the right people. Also give you active nudges on who you could connect to each other.
